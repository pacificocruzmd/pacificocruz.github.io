About

Trained at the source of the technique he practices

Dr. Pacifico Cruz is a board-certified plastic surgeon who moves fluently between reconstructive microsurgery and aesthetic facial surgery — work most practices keep apart — with regenerative technique carrying the same precision across both.

Dr. Pacifico Cruz completed his MD at the University of the Philippines College of Medicine in 2015, then residency in the Divisions of Plastic Surgery and Burns at UP–Philippine General Hospital (2016–2022) — a foundation in reconstruction, burn care and lymphedema surgery that both his later fellowships build on. He is a Diplomate of the Philippine Board of Plastic Surgery (2024).

He completed two fellowships: a full year under Dr. Rei Ogawa at Nippon Medical School in Tokyo (2024–2025), whose work in reconstructive surgery, microsurgery, keloid management and lymphedema is internationally recognised; and, in 2025, aesthetic plastic surgery under Dr. Patrick Tonnard and Dr. Alexis Verpaele in Ghent and at E:MC2 in Sint-Martens-Latem, Belgium (March–June; MACS facelift, lipofilling, nanofat), with additional training in Brussels.

He consults across partner hospitals in Metro Manila, with his flagship lymphedema surgery consultancy at St. Luke's Medical Center, BGC.

Technique Provenance

Trained directly under the surgeons whose techniques he practices

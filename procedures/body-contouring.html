The idea

Body contouring covers a range of operations that change shape and proportion rather than treat the skin or face. What is right depends on what is actually driving the concern — localized fat, skin laxity after weight change or pregnancy, or volume that surgery alone can restore — and each of those points toward a different procedure.

A plan usually starts by ruling things out rather than in: whether the concern is realistically addressable through surgery, what a result looks like on your own tissue and frame, and how the recovery fits your life. What is offered here works alongside, not instead of, the regenerative and injectable side of the practice.

Not every patient is a surgical candidate

Surgery solves problems non-surgical care cannot, but it is not automatically the better answer. Part of the consultation is an honest read on whether it is warranted at all, and if so, which of these operations actually addresses what you want changed.
